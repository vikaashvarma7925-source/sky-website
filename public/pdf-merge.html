PDF Merge

Join two or more PDFs into one file, in the order you choose.

How to use

Tap Choose PDFs again to add more files.

Files join top to bottom. Use the arrows to move a file, ✕ to remove it.

Your PDFsnever leave your device

Privacy note: this tool runs fully in your browser. Your PDFs are never sent to SKY or anywhere else. Password-protected PDFs cannot be merged.
