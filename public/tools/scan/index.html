SKY Studio tool

Photo scanner

Take a photo of a paper. Straighten it and clean it so it looks like a scan.

Step 1. Drag the 4 round dots to the 4 corners of the paper. If the paper is sideways, turn the photo first. Any order is fine; the numbers show how the page will be placed.

Step 2. Check the result. If a corner is wrong, move the dot and press the button again.

Download PNG Download JPG

The photo stays on your device and is not uploaded. It is turned upright like your phone shows it and is shrunk if larger than 2400 pixels on its long side. The paper shape is what you choose (A4 ratio, which Letter paper is close to). "Guess from the 4 dots" cannot know the real paper shape and can look stretched when the photo is taken at a steep angle. Check straight lines and text edges. Cleaning cannot add detail that the photo does not have; shadows, glare and blur can stay. When you press Download, the file is saved on your device like any other download. For a PDF, use Photos to PDF with the saved picture.
