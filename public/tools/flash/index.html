SKY Studio tool

Flashcard maker

Type your cards, practise on this page, or print cards to cut and fold.

Printed cards have the front on the left and the back on the right. Cut each card out and fold it along the dotted line. Everything you type stays in this page. It is not uploaded or saved, so it is gone when you close or reload the page. A saved PDF is stored on your device like any other file.
