SKY Studio tool

Habit tracker

Make a one-month habit sheet. Tick the days on this page, or print it and tick with a pen.

Ticks belong to the habit name (capital letters do not matter). If you change a name, its ticks stay with the old name and do not move to the new one.

Everything stays in this page. Nothing is uploaded or saved, so ticks are gone when you close or reload the page. Save the PDF if you want to keep a sheet. A saved PDF is stored on your device like any other file.
