SKY Studio tool

Menu maker

Type your shop name, groups and items with prices. Check the preview, then print or save as PDF. Empty lines are left out.

Everything you type stays in this page. It is not uploaded or saved. Prices are printed exactly as you type them. The tool does not calculate or check them. Limits: 6 groups, 15 items in each. A long menu continues on more pages. When you save a PDF, it is stored on your device like any other file.
