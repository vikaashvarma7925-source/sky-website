SKY Studio tool

Study timetable

Make a weekly timetable, fill the boxes, then print it on one A4 page.

Type in the boxes below. Each box can hold up to 20 characters.

Everything you type stays in this page. It is not uploaded or saved, so it is gone when you close the page. When you save a PDF, it is stored on your device like any other file. The fill button only places your subjects in turn. It does not plan your study for you.
