SKY Studio tool

Image format converter

Change photos between JPG, PNG and WebP.

Photos stay on your device and are not uploaded. Each photo is turned upright like your phone shows it. Animated pictures are not kept: only the first frame is converted. Picture details such as date and location are not kept in the new file. Transparent parts become white when you change to JPG. PNG is not lossy, so it can be bigger than the original. Changing a JPG or WebP again and again lowers quality each time. When you press Download, the file is saved on your device like any other download.
