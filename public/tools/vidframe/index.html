SKY Studio tool

Video frame saver

Pick a moment in a video, crop it if you want, and save that frame as a picture.

The video stays on your device and is not uploaded. The picture has the same size as the video picture, or the cropped part of it. It is saved on your device like any other download. This tool saves a single picture. It does not cut the video itself. Use the buttons to move to the exact moment, then check the preview before you save.
