SKY Studio tool

Contacts CSV to VCF

Turn a contacts spreadsheet (CSV) into a file your phone can import.

Download contacts.vcf

Your file is read in this page. It is not uploaded or saved. Not tested on every phone: after download, open the file on your phone and check one or two contacts before trusting the rest. Save the CSV as UTF-8 (in Excel: CSV UTF-8) so Hindi names stay correct; other encodings are refused. Phone numbers are saved as plain phone numbers with no mobile or home label. Columns used: Name or First/Last Name, Phone, Phone 2, Email, Company.
