SKY Studio tool

Split PDF

Take some pages out of a PDF, or cut it into several smaller files.

What do you want?

The file stays on your device and is not uploaded. Pages are copied as they are. Bookmarks, form fields and links inside the original may not carry over. Your original file is not changed. Each new file is saved on your device like any other download. Open each one and check the pages before you send it.
