SKY Studio tool

Audio cutter and joiner

Choose one or more audio files, set where each one starts and ends, and save one joined WAV file.

Download

Files stay on your device and are not uploaded. The result is a WAV file (44.1 kHz, 16 bit, stereo), which is bigger than MP3. It is saved on your device like any other download. The joined result can be up to 5 minutes long, otherwise it is refused. Use audio you own or have permission to edit.
