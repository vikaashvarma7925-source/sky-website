SKY Studio tool

Video to GIF

Make a short silent GIF from a part of a video.

Tip: start with 3 seconds, medium width and 10 pictures a second. Long, wide or smooth GIFs can be slow on a phone or an older computer. If the GIF is too heavy, this page tells you to make it smaller. The video stays on your device and is not uploaded. The GIF has no sound. A GIF can show only 256 colours, so photos can look a little rough, and a GIF file is much bigger than the same video. It is saved on your device like any other download. Open it and check it before you share it.
