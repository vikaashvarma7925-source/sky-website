SKY Studio tool

Certificate maker

Type the name and the words, check the preview, then print or save as PDF. It prints on one landscape A4 page.

Everything you type stays in this page. It is not uploaded or saved. This tool only arranges your words for events, classes, teams and shops. Do not use it to copy an official certificate, a degree or a government document, or to put someone else's name or seal on it. When you save a PDF, it is stored on your device like any other file.
