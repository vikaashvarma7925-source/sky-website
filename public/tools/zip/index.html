Files stay on your device and are not uploaded. When you open a ZIP, nothing is saved until you press a Download link, and then the file is saved on your device like any other download. Every file taken out of a ZIP is checked against the ZIP's own size and checksum, and a file that does not match is not offered. Only open ZIP files from people you trust, and scan downloaded files with your own security app before you open them.
