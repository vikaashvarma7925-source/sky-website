SKY Studio tool

Photos to PDF

Put photos or scans into one PDF, one photo per page.

Download PDF

Photos stay on your device and are not uploaded. Each photo is turned upright (like your phone shows it), shrunk if larger than 3508 pixels on its long side, and saved inside the PDF as a JPEG at high quality, so it is not a pixel-exact copy. Transparent parts of PNG files become white. When you press Download, the PDF is saved on your device like any other download. Open it once and check every page before you send it.
