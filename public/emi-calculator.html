EMI Calculator

Estimate for standard fixed-rate loans.

How to use

Standard reducing-balance EMI with a fixed rate, the way banks calculate most home, car and personal loans. Flat-rate or irregular loans work differently. Processing fees and insurance are not included. This tool does math only; confirm the final EMI with your bank.
