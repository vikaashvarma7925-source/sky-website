SKY CREATIVE TOOLS

Screenshot Text Editor

Upload a photo, cover an area, add text, then save your edited image. Your photo stays in this browser in this basic version.

Editing tools

After upload, text lines are found on your device automatically. Tap a gold box on the image, change the words, done. Font and colour are matched automatically (approximate, not exact).

Use the gold “Save PNG” button under the image. If you changed words but did not tap Replace, saving applies your change. The saved image goes to your phone’s Downloads or Files app.

More options (manual tools)

Manual mode: choose “Place / move text”, then tap the image to position the selected text. Drag existing text to move it. Choose “Cover old text” and drag across an area to cover it with a solid colour. Complex backgrounds are not reconstructed automatically. Your photo is never uploaded.
