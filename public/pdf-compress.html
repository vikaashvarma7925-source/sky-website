PDF Compress

Make photo-heavy PDFs smaller so they send faster.

How to use

How it works: pages become photos at lower quality. Text in the new file cannot be selected or searched. Works best on scanned documents and photo PDFs; text PDFs may not shrink.

Your PDFnever leaves your device

Privacy note: this tool runs fully in your browser. Your PDF is never sent to SKY or anywhere else. The compressed copy keeps the page look but pages become images.
