GST Calculator

Add or remove GST from any amount, instantly.

How to use

Add GST: you have a base price and want the final price with tax. Remove GST: you have a tax-inclusive price and want the base price back. Current common GST rates are 5% and 18% (40% for some luxury or sin goods) since 22 September 2025; older 12% and 28% slabs were removed. This tool does math only, not tax advice. Which rate applies to your item is your call; check cbic.gov.in.
