Privacy - SKY Studio

What this site runs: SKY Studio has no accounts, no forms, no uploads, and no comment or messaging features, so there is nowhere on the site to submit personal information. The tools on this site work the same way: Photo to KB and the image converter open a photo from your device and change it entirely inside your own browser; the contacts converter reads a spreadsheet you choose and creates the import file in your browser; the PDF unlock reads a PDF you choose and removes its open-password inside your own browser, and the password you type is used only in the page and never saved; and the word counter, duplicate remover, lucky draw, teleprompter, handwriting sheet maker, OMR sheet maker, label maker, invoice maker and resume maker process only the text and settings you enter in the page. The Photos to PDF, Merge PDFs, ZIP maker and opener, biodata maker, menu maker and certificate maker work the same way: your photos, PDFs, ZIP files and typed details are read and changed inside your own browser, and when you download or print a result it is saved on your device like any other download. None of it is uploaded, sent anywhere, or saved. The site itself uses no analytics and no tracking cookies, and every font, image, and video is served from this site rather than loaded from third-party services.

How the visuals work: the moving effects (cards, ripples, scene videos) run inside your own browser. Your touches and scrolls are processed on your device and are not sent back by the site's code.

Private previews: before launch, preview links are protected by an access cookie that only checks whether you may view the preview. It is not used to track you, and it is not part of the public site.

Hosting: the site is served by Cloudflare's network. As with any website, the hosting provider processes your connection (which can include your IP address) to deliver pages to you.

Privacy questions: write to SKY Studio at skystudio.feedback@gmail.com.
