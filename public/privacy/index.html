Privacy - SKY Studio

Accounts and forms: SKY Studio has no accounts and no sign-up, and there are no comment, messaging or contact forms on the site, so there is no account or profile information to give. The only way to reach us is the email address at the bottom of this page.

How the tools handle your files and text: every tool runs inside your own browser. When a tool asks you to choose a file - a photo, a PDF, a spreadsheet or a ZIP - that file is opened and changed by your browser on your own device; it is not uploaded to any server. The same is true for things you type into a tool, like resume, invoice or menu details, or the password you enter in the PDF unlock tool: they are processed in the page on your device, not sent to a server.

What is saved: the site has no server-side storage for tool data, so it does not keep copies of your files or of what you type. When you press Download or Print, the result is saved on your own device like any other download, and that copy belongs to you.

Analytics and tracking: the site uses no analytics and no tracking cookies. Every font, image and video is served from this site, not loaded from third-party services.

How the visuals work: the moving effects (cards, ripples, scene videos) run inside your own browser. Your touches and scrolls are processed on your device and are not sent back by the site's code.

AURA, the AI assistant (preview): when you chat with AURA, your messages are sent to this site's server, which passes them to the AI provider shown in the chat (today: Cloudflare Workers AI, running on Cloudflare's network) to write an answer. Other listed providers (Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral) are not connected today, so nothing is sent to them. AURA's conversation history is stored only in your own browser, and you can delete it anytime with "Clear my history" on the AURA page; the site keeps no server-side copy of your chats. Voice input, where your browser supports it, uses your browser's speech recognition feature: your browser may send the recorded audio to its own service (for example Google in Chrome) to turn it into text, under your browser's own privacy terms. It starts only when you tap the microphone button. Read-aloud uses your device's built-in voices. Please do not share passwords, OTPs or card details in chat.

Hosting: the site is served by Cloudflare's network. As with any website, the hosting provider handles the technical connection data needed to deliver pages to you, which can include your IP address; those connection records are handled under Cloudflare's own policies, not stored by this site.

Privacy questions: write to SKY Studio at skystudio.feedback@gmail.com.
