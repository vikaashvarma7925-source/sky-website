Honest limits

  • Full expressions, gestures and real lip sync (your example video's biggest feature): not implemented yet. This preview uses a static reference photo. It has no blinking, mouth movement or gestures.
  • Emotions are voice-only today. Each reply carries a tone label describing AURA's own reply (happy, concerned, thinking...) and the voice adjusts a little; her face does not change expression yet. The label describes her answer, never a guess about your mood.
  • No agent actions yet. She cannot browse the web, read files or control external apps. The explicit calculator and text-analysis tools above work, but she cannot invoke them autonomously. External actions are disabled, not merely hidden behind a confirmation button.
  • Voice quality depends on the browser's built-in voices - standard system voices, not a studio voice or a match verified against your reference.
