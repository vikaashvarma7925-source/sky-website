GUIDES

Document guides. The official way, simplified.

These guides only show you the way. Applications always happen on the official portal; this website does not issue any document. Fees and rules can change - this information was checked against official sources on 24 October 2026. Always re-check the current details on the relevant official site before applying.

GUIDE / PAN

How to get your first PAN Card

Route 1: Instant e-PAN (free)

A pre-login service of the Income Tax Department's e-Filing portal. For this: you must not already have a PAN, you need a valid Aadhaar and a mobile number linked to that Aadhaar, and the applicant must not be a minor.

  1. Open the e-Filing portal - click "Instant e-PAN" on the homepage
  2. Click "Get New e-PAN" and enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
  3. Enter the 6-digit OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile (the OTP stays valid for 15 minutes)
  4. Validate your Aadhaar details
  5. Once the request is submitted, you can check the status from the same page and download the e-PAN PDF

This service gives a digitally signed e-PAN in electronic format, free of cost. No physical card is sent.

Route 2: Regular PAN application (physical card or e-PAN)

There are two official portals: Protean e-Gov or UTIITSL.

  • Since April 2026, Form 93 (individuals) / Form 94 (non-individual entities) applies for new PANs for Indian citizens; Form 95/96 for foreign citizens.
  • In paperless mode (Aadhaar e-KYC + e-Sign) you do not need to send physical documents; payment is by credit card, debit card or net banking.
  • Fees (on Protean's site, taxes included): paperless application with e-PAN by email only Rs 66; paperless application with physical card at an Indian address Rs 101; in the mode where physical documents are sent, e-PAN Rs 72, physical card Rs 107.
  • After applying, you can track the status on the same portal using the acknowledgement number.

Documents (for Form 93, from Protean's official list)

  • An Aadhaar card works as proof of both identity and address.
  • Note: Aadhaar is not accepted as proof of date of birth. For DOB proof, Protean's site lists documents such as passport, driving licence, voter ID card or a matriculation certificate/marksheet from a recognized board.
  • For individuals born on or after 1 October 2023, a birth certificate (municipal authority/Registrar of Births & Deaths) is mandatory.
  • Giving the Aadhaar number in the application is mandatory (except for exempt categories).
  • If you want the e-PAN by email, an email ID is required.
  • The name on your PAN card will be printed as per Aadhaar, so the name must match Aadhaar.

GUIDE / DRIVING LICENCE

How to get your first Driving Licence

A licence is made in two steps: first a Learner's Licence (LL), then the permanent Driving Licence (DL). Applications happen on the national Sarathi portal - selecting your state there is required, because the process, fees and test method can differ slightly by state.

Age (Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, Section 4)

  • For a light motor vehicle such as a geared motorcycle or a car: you must have completed 18 years.
  • Gearless motorcycle/moped up to 50cc: 16 years, with written consent from parents/guardian.
  • Transport (commercial) vehicles have separate, stricter conditions; if you want that category, check its conditions on the Sarathi portal.

Step 1: Learner's Licence

  • Select your state on the Sarathi portal and fill Form 2.
  • Documents: age proof, address proof, passport size photo; for those under 40, Form 1 (self-declaration of physical fitness), for 40 or above, medical certificate Form 1A (as per the Parivahan FAQ).
  • The LL test is on traffic signs and rules. Many states offer an online LL test from home with Aadhaar authentication (faceless service) - Maharashtra is on the official list.
  • Central fees: LL Rs 150 per vehicle class + test fee Rs 50. State fees can differ; as an example, the Maharashtra Transport Department's site lists LL Rs 151 + test Rs 50.
  • The LL stays valid for 6 months.

Step 2: Permanent Driving Licence

  • Apply for the DL 30 days after getting the LL, and before the LL's validity ends.
  • A driving test slot is booked on the Sarathi portal; the test is taken by driving a vehicle at the RTO.
  • Central fees: driving test Rs 300 + DL issue Rs 200 (state smart card charges can differ; example: Maharashtra's site lists Rs 716 for the full permanent licence).
  • The licence is issued after passing the test. If you fail, a re-test can be taken after 7 days (Parivahan FAQ).

GUIDE / VOTER ID

How to get your first Voter ID (EPIC)

Who can apply (as per ECI)

  • Indian citizen
  • Has completed 18 years on the qualifying date (1 January, 1 April, 1 July or 1 October)
  • An ordinary resident of the constituency where registration is sought
  • Not already enrolled in a voter list anywhere else

What goes with Form 6 (from the current Form 6's official list)

  • Photo: recent passport size colour photo, 4.5cm x 3.5cm, white background.
  • Aadhaar details: in item 5 of Form 6, either fill in the Aadhaar number, or tick the box "I am not able to furnish my Aadhaar Number because I don't have Aadhaar Number". In other words, not having Aadhaar does not block registration.
  • Age proof (any one, self-attested copy): birth certificate (municipal authority/Registrar of Births & Deaths), Aadhaar card, PAN card, driving licence, Class X or XII certificate from CBSE/ICSE/State board (if it has DOB), Indian passport.
  • Address proof (any one, self-attested copy; in the applicant's name or in the name of parents/spouse/adult child already enrolled at the same address): water/electricity/gas connection bill for that address (at least 1 year), Aadhaar card, current passbook of a nationalized/scheduled bank or post office, Indian passport, land records from the Revenue Department (Kisan Bahi), registered rent/lease deed (for tenants), registered sale deed (for own house).
  • If you have none of these documents, there is an "any other document" option; in such cases field verification is always done.

Steps (online, free)

  1. Go to voters.eci.gov.in or use the Voter Helpline app (Android/iOS).
  2. Create an account, log in, choose "New voter registration (Form 6)".
  3. Fill in the details, upload photo and documents, and submit.
  4. Save the application reference number - status is tracked with it.
  5. A Booth Level Officer (BLO) may verify the address, so keep documents ready.
  6. After approval, the e-EPIC (digital voter card) is downloaded from voters.eci.gov.in; the physical EPIC is sent by speed post free of cost.
  • Fees: registration and EPIC are both completely free.
  • Status: "Track application status" on voters.eci.gov.in or the Voter Helpline app. Whether a name is already in the voter list: electoralsearch.eci.gov.in. Helpline: 1950.
  • Note: giving a false declaration in Form 6 is a punishable offence under law (Representation of the People Act, 1950, Section 31).
Official links: Voters' portal Electoral search

GUIDE / AADHAAR

How to get Aadhaar (new enrolment)

Aadhaar enrolment is free of cost, but it is not fully online: your photo, fingerprints and iris scan must be captured in person. You can go to any authorized Aadhaar enrolment centre or Aadhaar Seva Kendra anywhere in India.

Steps

  1. Optional but useful: book a free appointment at bookappointment.uidai.gov.in (one person can book up to 2 appointments a month). Walk-in also works at centres.
  2. Carry a Proof of Identity and a Proof of Address. UIDAI accepts a wide range: voter ID, passport, driving licence, ration card; address proofs also include recent electricity/water/landline bills. The full list is on the UIDAI site. If someone has no documents, a Head of Family who already has Aadhaar can introduce other family members.
  3. At the centre, the enrolment form is filled, your details and biometrics are captured, and you get an acknowledgement slip with your Enrolment ID (EID). Keep this slip safe - status is checked with it.
  4. The Aadhaar letter comes by post. Once generated, the e-Aadhaar PDF can also be downloaded from the UIDAI website.

Children

  • Children of any age, including newborns, can be enrolled. For children below 5, no fingerprints/iris are taken; the child's Aadhaar is linked to a parent's/guardian's Aadhaar.
  • Two mandatory biometric updates are needed - at age 5 and again at age 15 - and these updates are free.

Already have Aadhaar? Updates are a different process

  • Document (identity/address proof) update: Rs 75 at an Aadhaar centre; free on the myAadhaar portal till 14 June 2027 (as listed on UIDAI's Aadhaar Seva Kendra page, checked 25 October 2026).
  • Enrolment is free. If anyone charges you for new enrolment, you can complain on UIDAI's toll-free helpline 1947.
Official links: UIDAI enrolment Book appointment myAadhaar portal

GUIDE / BIRTH CERTIFICATE

How to register a birth and get the certificate

Under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969, every birth must be registered with the local Registrar - the municipal corporation/council in towns, or the Gram Panchayat in villages. Registration within 21 days of the birth is free. In most hospitals the hospital itself reports the birth; confirm this and collect the birth report.

Steps

  1. Get the birth report from the hospital. For a home birth, the family informs the local Registrar.
  2. Fill the birth registration form with your local Registrar. Many states and cities let you apply online on the national Civil Registration System (CRS) portal or on your state/municipal portal - but the exact route differs by state and local body, so check your own locality's process. There is no single nationwide online application for every area.
  3. After registration, collect the certificate from the Registrar's office; in many areas it can also be downloaded online through the CRS portal.

If the 21-day window has passed (Section 13 of the Act)

  • Within 30 days: registration with a late fee.
  • 30 days to 1 year: written permission of the District Registrar or the prescribed authority, a late fee, and a self-attested document.
  • After 1 year: only on an order of the District Magistrate, Sub-Divisional Magistrate or an authorized Executive Magistrate, with a fee.
  • Late fees and the exact process vary by state.

Documents usually asked

  • The hospital birth report, parents' identity and address proofs, and the marriage certificate where applicable. The exact list varies by local body, so confirm with your Registrar.
Official links: CRS portal Civil Registration System

GUIDE / AYUSHMAN CARD

Ayushman card (PM-JAY): check eligibility first

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY gives health cover of Rs 5 lakh per family per year for hospital treatment to poor and vulnerable families identified through the SECC 2011 deprivation/occupation criteria; many states have expanded coverage to more groups. Not everyone qualifies - checking eligibility is the first step.

Steps

  1. Check eligibility on the beneficiary portal beneficiary.nha.gov.in (mobile + OTP login), or in the Ayushman App, or call the helpline 14555, or ask at the nearest empanelled hospital or Common Service Centre (CSC).
  2. If eligible, complete the Aadhaar-based eKYC at the hospital, CSC or through the app.
  3. The Ayushman card is generated and can be downloaded. Treatment at empanelled hospitals is cashless.

Keep in mind

  • The card is completely free. No fee, no agent needed. Do not pay anyone who offers to make an Ayushman card for money.
  • ABHA is different: the ABHA number (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) is a voluntary digital health ID for your health records. It is not insurance and gives no treatment cover; making an ABHA ID does not make you eligible for Ayushman benefits.
Official links: Beneficiary portal About PM-JAY PM-JAY site
Video (official channel - Ayushman Bharat, Uttar Pradesh): "Ayushman Bharat yojana ki patrata jaanchein aur card banana sunishchit karein" The process shown is the national PM-JAY one; eligibility rules in your state may add more groups.

Important safety tips

  • Apply only on official websites. The links given in this guide are the official ones - alongside government sites (like incometax.gov.in, parivahan.gov.in, voters.eci.gov.in), for PAN these include the government-appointed agencies Protean e-Gov (tinpan.proteantech.in) and UTIITSL (pan.utiitsl.com).
  • Beware of fake sites with similar names in search results or ads; check the URL carefully.
  • Never give your Aadhaar number, OTP or photos of your documents to any agent or stranger.
  • This website only provides information; the government issues the document.
